Length Contraction — Moving Rulers Are Shorter

A 1-metre ruler at rest has proper length L₀. When that same ruler moves at velocity v relative to an observer, its length as measured by simultaneous endpoint readings in the observer’s frame is

L = L₀ · √(1 − v²/c²) = L₀ / γ

This is length contraction. It is real (not an optical illusion) and follows directly from the Lorentz transformation. Drag the velocity slider to see how the moving ruler is measured shorter than its proper length.
