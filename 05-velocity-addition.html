Relativistic Velocity Addition

In Galilean physics, if you throw a ball at speed u′ from a train moving at speed v, the ball’s speed in the ground frame is simply u = u′ + v. With Lorentz transformations, this fails. The correct law is

u = (u′ + v) / (1 + u′ v / c²)

The denominator caps the result at c: even if you flash light (u′ = c) from a train moving at v, the light still travels at c in the ground frame.
