Minkowski Spacetime Diagram — Two Frames

This is a Minkowski (spacetime) diagram: time runs vertically, space runs horizontally, both in units where c = 1 so that light rays make 45° angles. Two reference frames are overlaid:

  • Frame S (rest frame): orthogonal axes x and ct.
  • Frame S′ (moving at velocity v relative to S): tilted axes x′ and ct′. The ct′ axis is the worldline of the S′ observer (slope = c/v); the x′ axis is the line of "S′-simultaneity" (slope = v/c).
  • Light cone: grey diagonals at ±45°, marking the trajectories of light. These are the same in every frame (Einstein’s 2nd postulate).

Click anywhere in the diagram to place an event; the simulation reports its (t, x) coordinates in S and (t′, x′) coordinates in S′, computed via the Lorentz transformation.
